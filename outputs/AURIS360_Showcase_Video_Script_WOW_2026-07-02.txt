AURIS360 WOW showcase video script

Purpose:
Create an inspiring 4-6 minute product showcase for prospective pilot clients.

Creative direction:
Premium, energetic, modern HSE technology. Use screen recordings of the app where possible, mixed with animated zooms on dashboard metrics, module chips and workflow transitions.

Suggested structure:

1. Opening impact
On screen: AURIS360 hero slide / dashboard cockpit.
Voiceover: "Imagine an HSE system where every incident, observation, permit, action, legal duty and evidence file is connected."

2. The problem
On screen: Fragmented records moving into one AURIS360 platform.
Voiceover: "Most companies do not lack HSE effort. They lack visibility. AURIS360 brings that effort into one controlled operating system."

3. The ecosystem
On screen: Module universe slide, zooming through Incidents, BBS, PTW, Risk, Legal, Chemicals, Audit Trail, AI and future modules.
Voiceover: "AURIS360 is not one register. It is a full HSE ecosystem covering safety, health, environment, compliance, assets, governance and intelligence."

4. Figures and dashboards
On screen: Dashboard metrics and charts.
Voiceover: "The platform turns data into decisions: open incidents, overdue actions, training compliance, legal score, chemical exposure, permits and audit evidence."

5. Field to boardroom
On screen: Report incident, add evidence, assign action, verify closure, show executive dashboard.
Voiceover: "A worker reports from the field. The HSE manager investigates. Actions are assigned. Evidence is retained. Leadership sees the risk picture."

6. Governance proof
On screen: Approval Center and Audit Trail.
Voiceover: "Every serious system must answer the auditor's question: who did what, when, and what changed?"

7. AI and future roadmap
On screen: Ask AURIS, predictive risk, specialist RA, fleet, ATEX, RCD, integrations and portals.
Voiceover: "AURIS AI and the roadmap make the platform even more powerful, moving clients from reactive compliance to predictive prevention."

8. Closing
On screen: Final brand slide.
Voiceover: "AURIS360. Visible risk. Controlled action. Proved compliance."
