# KPI export and recorded-trend consistency

9 September 2026. Follow-up to the tested combined preview #101 at `0ac5e08a69b7e8aad471e55a16b01cba128ae4dc`. Preview only; no production or database release is implied.

## Changes

- CSV export now evaluates indicators at the published compilation cut-off, matching the scorecard and year summary. Previously it used the reporting month, which could export excluded open-month results or an uncompiled quarterly/annual result.
- Reports explicitly state the compilation month/year, or that no month is compiled. CSV retains its existing twelve columns, filename, whole-register scope, quoting and download cleanup. Missing results remain blank; recorded zero remains zero. Selected past/future years and annual backdating retain the established evaluator rules.
- The small row chart is relabelled **Recorded trend** in the scorecard, column chooser and monthly table. It has always drawn January–December recorded values, not six months. Accessible SVG labels and native tooltips now disclose the selected year, full-year scope and possible open/future recorded periods. A dash explains that at least two results are needed. Chart points, colours, scaling and stored values are unchanged; these charts are not performance scores.
- Report Print/Export actions have a scoped 44px minimum height. Browser inspection found a legacy important mobile rule shrinking them to 42px; the narrowly scoped important minimum prevents that override without changing other module controls.

No scoring formula, threshold, approval, permission, source assignment, monthly-entry, print implementation or reporting-calendar policy is changed. JavaScript/CSS cache key: `20260909-reporting-consistency-1`; the configuration script keeps its existing key. The manifest retains 180 offline routes and all 22 migrations are unchanged. No SQL or dependency changes.
