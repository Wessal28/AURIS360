New Risk Assessment — Choose type
📋
Baseline / Generic RA
Full baseline assessment with prohibited categories, before/after risk scoring using Kinney method, source identification and action plan. Best for ongoing workplace hazard management.
Department-based Full scoring Action plan
🔧
Task-Based RA
Step-by-step task analysis identifying hazards per work step. Before/after risk scoring per step with controls. Best for specific tasks, maintenance, non-routine work.
Task steps Per-step scoring PPE required
⚡
Dynamic RA
Rapid field assessment for real-time hazard identification. Simplified 4-level scoring (Low/Medium/High/Critical). Best for emergency work, changing conditions, on-the-spot decisions.
Field use Quick scoring Validity period
